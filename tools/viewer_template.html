How to read this

The blind window. The red tail is the last 5 s, when the IMU outputs only zeros. Path Finder holds position there, because the velocity estimate just before the failure is the least reliable of the flight.

The ±1σ band is the smoother's own uncertainty, from the optional --sigma output. It is calibrated by leave-one-sequence-out cross-validation, and 67% of held-out errors fall inside it. Large errors are more common than a Gaussian would predict: 84% fall inside 2σ, against an expected 95%.

Not shown: textbook strapdown integration of the same inputs ends about 350 m to 50 km away, so it is listed in the table instead of drawn.

Data: EuRoC MAV and TUM-VI public datasets, degraded with the organisers' degrade_imu.py (seed 42). H1 reading shown in the plots. Generated by tools/export_viewer_data.py.
